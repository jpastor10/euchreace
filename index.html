Learn to play, and play better

Euchre Ace is the friendly way to learn Euchre and keep getting better at it, hand after hand.

Get Euchre Ace on the App Store

Most card apps just deal the cards. Euchre Ace plays alongside you. A built in coach watches every decision and offers a clear, plain spoken tip at the moments that matter: when to order up, when to go alone, which card to lead, and when to hold your bower. You are never put on the spot. The guidance is there when you want it, and quiet when you do not.

Whether you are picking up the game for the first time or you have played at the kitchen table for years, Euchre Ace meets you where you are and helps you play smarter.

What you get

A coach that teaches as you play

Short, specific tips explain not just what to play but why, so the lesson sticks and your game improves on its own.

Guided tutorials when you want to go deeper

Short lessons on individual topics, each paired with a live walk through of a sample hand, so you can study one part of the game away from the table.

Opponents that play a real game

Choose your difficulty. The easier levels are patient and forgiving. Expert plays a sharp, honest game that will push you.

Bidding practice

A focused trainer that grades your bids against an expert read of the hand, so you can sharpen the part of Euchre that decides most games.

Progress you can see

Follow your skill over time with clear stats, including a decision quality score that rewards good choices rather than lucky cards.

A hand recap

After a hand, see the one moment that mattered most and what a stronger play would have looked like.

Game Center leaderboards

Compare your wins, streaks, loners, and more with players everywhere.

House rules to match your table

Turn on options like Stick the Dealer, Farmer's Hand, Ace No Face, Canadian Loner and more so the game plays the way you play at home.

Plays anywhere

Fully offline. No account, no sign in, no waiting on a connection. Just deal and play.
