How to Play

The Deck

Euchre uses 24 cards: the 9, 10, J, Q, K, and Ace of all four suits. All other cards are removed from the deck.

Non-trump ranks: 9 < 10 < J < Q < K < A
Trump rank: 9 < 10 < Q < K < A < Left J < Right J

Trump & The Bowers

The trump suit outranks all others. Within trump, the card order from highest to lowest is:

  1. Right Bower (J♠*) (highest)
  2. Left Bower (J♣*)
  3. Ace of Trump
  4. King of Trump
  5. Queen of Trump
  6. 10 of Trump
  7. 9 of Trump (lowest trump)

* when Spades are trump

The Left Bower is the Jack of the same-color suit as trump. It becomes part of the trump suit for the entire hand.

Spades trump: J♠ (Right), J♣ (Left), A♠, K♠, Q♠, 10♠, 9♠

The Deal

Cards are dealt in two rounds starting left of the dealer. The deal alternates 3-2 or 2-3 so each player receives 5 cards. One card is then flipped face-up as the potential trump.

The dealer position rotates left each hand.

Bidding — Round 1

Starting left of the dealer, each player decides whether to accept the face-up card's suit as trump by saying "Order Up."

Order Up Trump is set. The dealer adds the card to their hand and discards one face-down.

Pass Move to the next player. If all four pass, the card is turned face-down.

You can go alone when ordering up. Your partner sits out — but if you win all 5 tricks you score 4 points.

Bidding — Round 2

After the card is turned down, players may name any suit except the turned-down suit as trump. The same pass order applies.

Name a Suit That suit becomes trump for the hand.

Pass Move to the next player.

Stick the Dealer (optional): the dealer cannot pass in round 2 and must call a suit.

Playing Tricks

The player left of the dealer leads the first card. Rules:

  • You must follow the led suit if you have one.
  • If you have no cards of the led suit, you may play any card.
  • The left bower counts as trump, not its original suit.
  • The highest card in the led suit wins unless trump is played in which case the highest trump wins.
  • The trick winner leads the next trick.

Scoring

SituationTeamPts
Calling team wins 3–4 tricksCallers+1
Calling team wins all 5 (march)Callers+2
Caller wins all 5 aloneCallers+4
Calling team wins < 3 (euchred)Opponents+2

The first team to reach 10 points wins the game.

Going Alone

When ordering up or calling a suit, you may announce you're going alone. Your partner's cards are set aside and you play all 5 tricks solo.

  • Win 3–4 tricks alone → 1 point (same as normal).
  • Win all 5 tricks alone → 4 points.
  • Win fewer than 3 tricks alone → euchred, opponents get 2 points (or 4 points optionally if you decide to play that way).
