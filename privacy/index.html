Euchre Ace Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Overview

Euchre Ace is designed to run locally on your device. It does not require an account and does not collect or transmit your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or other personal information.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app may save gameplay preferences, sound and haptic settings, learning settings, statistics, and resume-game information so the game works properly between launches. This information is stored locally on your device. It may include a private record of moments where your play differed from a coaching tip, including the cards involved. That record stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Advertising

Euchre Ace is free to play and may show ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google may collect and process information such as a device advertising identifier, app usage information, and approximate location derived from your IP address, under Google's own privacy policy. In regions that require it, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are used. On Apple devices, you can control ad tracking through the App Tracking Transparency prompt and your device settings. You can review Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. If you unlock the optional Supporter Pack, ads are removed.

Purchases And Game Center

In app purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's privacy practices. When you are signed in to Game Center, the app submits ranked gameplay totals such as your wins and win percentage to Apple's Game Center so they can appear on leaderboards. Game Center is an Apple service, and Apple handles your Game Center identity and the scores it stores under Apple's own privacy practices. Only games played at the Expert level with standard house rules are submitted.

Sharing

The app does not sell personal information. The app does not share gameplay data with third parties automatically.

TestFlight Beta Builds

If you are testing Euchre Ace through TestFlight, the beta build may show a Report button during gameplay. Tapping Report copies the current hand data to your clipboard so you can paste it into a message or email if you choose to report an issue. The app does not send that hand data anywhere on its own. This applies only to beta builds and is not present in the public App Store release.

Children

Euchre Ace is a card game and teaching tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact the developer at euchreusupport@gmail.com.
