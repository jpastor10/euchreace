When to call trump in euchre

Calling trump is a promise that your team can take at least three of the five tricks. Here is what makes a hand worth that promise.

Trump strength comes first

The right bower is the best card in the game. The left bower is second, and the ace of trump is the next big control card.

Three trump is often enough to call. Two trump can be enough, but only when those two are strong or the rest of your hand gives them real help.

Side cards count too

Off suit aces can win tricks without spending trump. A lone ace in a suit is especially valuable, because it can win early and then leave you with no cards in that suit.

Short suits give you chances to trump in later. Weak side suits do the opposite. They leave you holding cards that probably cannot win unless your partner helps.

A hand with three suits is usually neutral. With no off suit ace, it becomes a weakness.

Your seat changes the risk

Ordering up when your partner is the dealer puts a trump in your partner's hand. Ordering up when an opponent is the dealer makes their hand stronger, so your own hand needs to be better.

In round two, calling "next" from first seat can be powerful. Next is the other suit of the same color as the card that was turned down. It still needs enough strength in your hand.

First seat: look ahead

The player to the dealer's left has a special advantage. Suppose you could order up the upcard, and you also hold a good hand in a different suit. Sometimes the better move is to pass.

  • If your partner calls the upcard suit, you hold a good supporting hand.
  • If an opponent calls it, you have a good chance to euchre them, since the burden of three tricks is on their team.
  • If everyone passes, you get the first pick in round two and can call your other strong suit.

That last case is the prize. The dealer's team normally gets to pick up a trump and discard to shorten a suit. By waiting, you still call trump and they never get that pickup.

Going alone

Going alone is for hands that can realistically take all five tricks. If a normal call already wins the game, taking the safer point is usually better than risking a euchre.

Rule of thumb: Call when you can see a path to three tricks: real trump strength, help from your side cards, or a seat that favors you.
