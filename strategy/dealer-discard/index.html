Euchre strategy

What to discard as dealer in euchre

When you pick up the upcard as dealer, you hold six cards and must put one back. A good discard can set up an extra trick.

Keep your trump

Once trump is called, trump cards are your strongest cards. Even a small trump can take an opponent's ace or stop a side suit winner.

Never discard trump while you still hold a card from another suit that you can let go.

Make a void if you can

Discarding your only card in a suit leaves you void in that suit. The next time that suit is led, you can trump in and take the trick.

Say diamonds are trump and the 9♥ is your only heart. Discard it, and the next heart lead gives you a chance to win the trick with a trump.

A void is often worth more than keeping a low off suit card.

Keep the ace, create the void

When you hold an ace and one small card in the same side suit, discard the small card. With A♦ and 9♦ in a side suit, the 9♦ goes.

The ace may win a trick by itself. After you play it, you are void in that suit and ready to trump in.

It is usually better to hang on to an ace than to discard it just to get short.

No lone card? Trim your shortest suit

When no side suit is down to one card, discard the lowest card in your shortest side suit, not the lowest card in your hand.

The lowest card of a longer suit may look weaker. But trimming the short suit moves you closer to a void, and that is worth more.

When every card is trump

If your whole hand is trump, you have to discard one. Let go of the lowest and keep your bowers, your ace and your other high trump.

Rule of thumb: Keep your trump, keep your aces, and use the discard to get short in a side suit.

Practice it with a coach

Euchre Ace points out moments like these while you play and explains the reason, so the idea sticks.
