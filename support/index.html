Euchre Ace support

Euchre Ace is made by one developer, and every message gets read.

Get in touch

Write to euchreusupport@gmail.com. It helps to mention your device, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem came up.

If the coach gave advice you think was wrong, please send that too. Those notes make the coach better.

Common questions

I bought the Supporter Pack and it is not showing.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Where are the house rules?
Open Settings and look for House Rules. A change applies to your next game.

How do I change how strong the computer players are?
Open Settings and choose a Difficulty.

Why are my games not on the leaderboards?
Leaderboards use Game Center, so you need to be signed in. Only games played at the Expert level with standard house rules are counted.

I am new to euchre. Where do I start?
Tap How to Play on the main menu for guided lessons.
